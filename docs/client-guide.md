# How to update your website

You can change the words, photos and details on your website yourself. No coding needed.

## Log in

1. Go to **your website address followed by /admin**, for example `vitalfascia.com.au/admin`.
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and log in with the GitHub account you were invited with.
3. You'll see a menu on the left: **Home page**, **Other pages** and **Business details & settings**.

## Change some text

1. Click **Home page** (or another group) on the left, then pick the section you want, for example *1. Top banner (Hero)*.
2. Click into a box and type your changes. Small grey hints under each box explain what it's for.
3. Click **Save** at the top right.
4. Wait about 1-2 minutes. Your live website updates automatically.

## Change or add a photo

1. Open the section with the photo (for example *Top banner* or *About*).
2. Click the photo box, then **Upload** to choose a picture from your computer, or pick one you've already uploaded.
3. **Always fill in "Photo description"**. This helps people using screen readers and helps Google. Describe the picture in one sentence.
4. Click **Save**.

Tip: use clear, bright photos. Large files are fine; the website shrinks them automatically.

## Add, remove or re-order items

Lists (services, testimonials, steps, menu links) show an **Add** button, a bin icon to delete, and a drag handle to re-order.

## Hide a section

Most home page sections have a **Show this section on the website** switch. Turn it off and save to hide the whole section.

## Update your contact details, hours and booking link

Go to **Business details & settings > Contact details & opening hours**. Every "Book" button on the site uses the **Online booking link** here.

## Change what Google shows

Go to **Business details & settings > Google search & social sharing (SEO)**. The *Page title* and *Page description* are what people see in Google results.

## Tips

- Only publish true information: prices, results and testimonials (with the person's permission).
- Changes can take 1-2 minutes to appear. Refresh the page if you don't see them.
- Made a mistake? Tell your web developer. Every change is saved and can be undone.
- Trouble logging in or something looks broken? Contact your web developer.
