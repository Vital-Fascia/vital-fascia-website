## Cancellation Policy

**We understand that life doesn’t always go to plan.**

Each appointment is time set aside for you, and when a session is cancelled at short notice, it often can’t be offered to someone else who needs care. This policy helps us keep appointments available for everyone, and keeps our fees fair for all clients.

### How it works

**12+ hours’ notice:** No charge. We’ll happily help you find a new time that suits.

**Between 4 and 12 hours’ notice:** A partial fee applies ($90). We’ll still do our best to offer your appointment to someone on our waitlist.

**4 hours’ notice or less, or no-show:** The full appointment fee applies, as we’re very unlikely to fill the time at this point.

### Cancellation fees

| Notice given                | Fee                  |
| --------------------------- | -------------------- |
| 12 hours or more            | No charge            |
| More than 4, less than 12 hours | $90              |
| 0–4 hours / No-show         | Full appointment fee |

### Good to know

- **How to cancel or reschedule:** Use the link in your confirmation email or reminder, or contact us directly at 0449 637 920.
- **Payment:** Any cancellation fee will be invoiced and must be settled before your next appointment.
- **Health fund rebates:** Cancellation fees can’t be claimed through private health insurance.
- **Late arrivals:** If you arrive late, we’ll do what we can, but your session may need to finish at the scheduled time so the next client isn’t affected. The full fee still applies.
- **Feeling unwell?** Please don’t push through. If you have a contagious illness, let us know as early as possible and we’ll work with you.
- **Genuine emergencies:** We know some things are out of anyone’s control. Unexpected situations are considered case by case, at our discretion.

**Thanks for helping us look after you, and every other client who relies on us.**

## Before Your First Visit — FAQ Copy

**Heading:** Before your first visit (FAQ)

#### What happens in my first session?

Your first session starts with a conversation. We’ll talk about what’s bringing you in, your history, and how your body feels day to day.

From there, we’ll look at how you move, stand and breathe. This helps us understand the patterns behind your tension, not just where you feel it.

Treatment is then tailored to you, combining hands-on myofascial and soft tissue work. You’ll leave with a clearer picture of what’s going on and one or two simple practices to support your progress between sessions.

#### What should I wear?

Wear something comfortable that you can move in, such as activewear or loose clothing. Shorts are helpful for any work around the hips and legs.

Some of your session may involve movement assessment, so clothing that lets you bend, reach and rotate freely is ideal. During hands-on treatment, you’ll only ever undress to the level you’re comfortable with, and you’ll be draped with towels throughout.

#### Can I claim on my health fund?

Yes. Sessions are claimable with most private health funds under extras cover.

How much you get back depends on your fund and level of cover, so we recommend checking with your provider beforehand. Please bring your health fund card to your appointment.

#### What is the cancellation policy?

We understand that life happens. If you need to change or cancel your booking, please give at least 12 hours’ notice so we can offer the time to someone else.

Cancellations with more than 4 but less than 12 hours’ notice incur a $90 fee. Cancellations with 0–4 hours’ notice, or no-shows, incur the full session fee.

Standard sessions are 60 minutes, which includes time for assessment, treatment and a short discussion of next steps.
